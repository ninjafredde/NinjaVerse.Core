NINJAVERSE · LIGHT CONE

THINGS TO TRY

  1. Light time gear: hold W. Stars ahead play fast-forward and stars behind play backwards, because you're out-running their old light.
  2. Space to freeze the clock, then click something far away. The fly-in replays exactly its last distance ÷ c years. Watch the strip.
  3. Gear 6 Horizon: the glow is first light (no light can reach you from further away). Press L for the "god view" with no light delay.
  4. Press T for the Motion time gear, then change gears. Every scale looks about equally alive.
Toy assumptions: flat 2D, static space (no expansion), c = 1 ly/yr. H hides this.
